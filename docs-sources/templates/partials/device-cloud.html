OpenMTP transfers files between your Mac and Android phones and tablets, Garmin watches and cycling computers, Meta Quest VR headsets, Kindle, BOOX and Supernote e-readers, Fujifilm and GoPro cameras, DJI controllers, MTP music players and voice recorders. It works with any device that shares its storage over MTP, though individual models can vary with the manufacturer's MTP implementation. How to connect your device to OpenMTP.
