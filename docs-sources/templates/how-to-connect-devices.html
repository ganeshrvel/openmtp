How to Transfer Files Between Mac and Android or Other MTP Devices

Most devices connect the same way. Start with the steps for every device, then check the instructions for your specific device if it doesn't work.

How to transfer files between every MTP device and Mac?

  1. Download OpenMTP and drag it into your Applications folder on your Mac.
  2. Connect the device to your Mac with a USB data cable. Some cables only charge.
  3. Unlock the device.
  4. If the device asks what the USB connection is for, choose File transfer (sometimes called MTP or Transfer files).
  5. Open the OpenMTP app. It has two panes: the files on your Mac are on the left and the MTP device is on the right. If it doesn't show up, click refresh on either pane.

Only one app can use an MTP device at a time. If OpenMTP can't see the device, quit anything that might be holding it: Preview, Image Capture, Photos, Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, Android File Transfer, Garmin Express, iMovie or Lightroom.

How to transfer files between Android phones and tablets and Mac?

Google Pixel, Motorola, OnePlus, Xiaomi, Redmi, Fairphone, Sony Xperia and other Android devices all use the same USB setting.

  1. Make sure the USB cable is properly connected.
  2. If the device isn't detected, try unplugging it and plugging it back in, and make sure your USB cable can transfer data, not just charge.
  3. Unlock your phone with its PIN, pattern or fingerprint.
  4. Pull down the notification shade and tap the USB notification.
  5. Under Use USB for, choose File transfer.
  6. No notification? Open your device's Settings, search for USB or USB Preferences, and choose File transfer there.

How to transfer files between Samsung phones and tablets and Mac?

  1. Make sure the USB cable is properly connected.
  2. If the device isn't detected, try unplugging it and plugging it back in, and make sure your USB cable can transfer data, not just charge.
  3. Unlock the phone. When it asks Allow access to phone data?, tap Allow.
  4. No prompt? Tap the USB notification and choose Transferring files.
  5. If OpenMTP goes blank, or the device isn't detected, when you connect, quit Samsung Smart Switch and try again. It can block other apps from reaching the phone over MTP.
  6. Still blank? Uninstalling Smart Switch has fixed the problem for some users. See how to remove Samsung Smart Switch and drivers from your Mac.
  7. Still stuck? Restart OpenMTP, quit any other app that might be holding the device, such as Kies, Preview, Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive or Lightroom, then see the troubleshooting guide.

How to transfer files between Meta Quest/Oculus VR headsets and Mac?

  1. Turn on the headset and log in.
  2. Connect the headset to your Mac with a USB-C data cable.
  3. Put the headset on. When it asks to allow access to data, choose Allow.
  4. No prompt? Open Notifications (the bell icon) on the Quest menu bar and look for a USB detected notification to trigger it.
  5. Open OpenMTP. The headset's storage appears on the right, so you can copy videos and screenshots off it, or copy music and custom maps onto it.

How to transfer files between Amazon Kindle e-readers and Mac?

Newer Kindles may use MTP when connected to a Mac, so they may not appear in Finder like a regular USB drive.

  1. Wake the Kindle and swipe past its lock screen or screensaver.
  2. Connect it to your Mac with a USB cable.
  3. Unlike Android, there's usually no menu to choose a USB mode. As long as the screen stays awake and unlocked, the Kindle exposes its storage automatically.
  4. Open OpenMTP. If the Kindle exposes its storage over MTP, it will appear on the right. Books and other documents are normally stored in the documents folder.

Older Kindles that appear in Finder as a drive don't need OpenMTP.

How to transfer files between Garmin watches and bike computers and Mac?

Some Garmins default to a proprietary Garmin Mode connection that macOS can't read, so you may need to switch the device to MTP first.

  1. Disconnect the watch or bike computer if it's plugged in.
  2. Hold the Up/Menu button to open the main menu, then go to System.
  3. Go to Advanced (on newer models such as the Fenix 8) or look for USB Mode directly, and change it from Garmin to MTP (Media Transfer Protocol).
  4. Quit Garmin Express if it's running. It holds on to the device.
  5. Connect the watch or bike computer with its USB cable.
  6. Open OpenMTP. If the device exposes its storage, you can browse and copy files normally. On compatible devices, GARMIN/NewFiles is used for courses, routes and similar files, and music-enabled watches expose a Music folder for music files.

How to transfer files between Fujifilm mirrorless cameras and Mac?

  1. Turn on the camera and press the MENU/OK button.
  2. Go to the Set Up menu (the wrench icon), then Connection Setting, then PC Connection Mode, and set it to USB Card Reader.
  3. Connect the camera to your Mac with a data-capable USB cable and turn it on.
  4. Open OpenMTP. Your photos and videos should be under the DCIM folder.

How to transfer files between DJI drone controllers and Mac?

  1. Turn on the controller and connect its USB-C port to your Mac.
  2. On the controller's touchscreen, swipe down from the very top edge twice to open the Android notification panel.
  3. Tap the notification that says Charging this device via USB or USB connection, then choose File Transfer or MTP.
  4. Open OpenMTP and browse the controller's internal or shared storage. Flight records are usually stored inside the DJI app's files, but the exact folder depends on the controller and DJI software version.

How to transfer files between MTP mp3 music players and voice recorders and Mac?

High-end players like these often run Android underneath, so the steps are close to a phone's.

  1. Unlock the player or recorder's screen.
  2. Connect it to your Mac.
  3. If it asks for a USB mode, choose MTP or File transfer. No prompt? Swipe down from the top of its screen and tap the USB connection setting, then change it from Charge Only or DAC Mode to MTP.
  4. Open OpenMTP and browse the folders on the device. Copy music or recordings to the folder used by your player or recorder.
