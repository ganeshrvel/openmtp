How to transfer files from Android to Mac

Three steps. No Wi-Fi setup, no account, no cloud.

  1. 1

    Download OpenMTP

    Get the build for your Mac, Apple Silicon or Intel, and drag OpenMTP into your Applications folder. Or install it with Homebrew.

  2. 2

    Connect your phone with a USB cable

    Unlock your phone, tap the USB notification and choose File transfer. OpenMTP detects it right away.

  3. 3

    Drag and drop your files

    Your Mac is on the left and your phone is on the right. Drag files across, or copy with ⌘C and paste with ⌘V.

Kalam Kernel

OpenMTP 3.0 introduced Kalam, a new MTP kernel written from scratch and named after Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam.

It copies files at 30 to 40 MB/s on low and mid-range devices and 100 to 120 MB/s on high-end devices, with no 4GB file size limit.

Is OpenMTP safe to use?

Yes. OpenMTP is open source under the MIT license, so anyone can read its code. Releases are signed and notarized by Apple, and the app doesn't collect personally identifiable information. Only download it from:

openmtp.ganeshrvel.com GitHub Homebrew

Is OpenMTP free?

PayPal Buy Me A Coffee

Is OpenMTP a free alternative to Google's Android File Transfer?

Yes. Google has discontinued its Android File Transfer app for Mac, and the popular alternatives are paid or come with restrictions. OpenMTP is free and open source, with no limits.

My phone isn't connecting, or I see a blank screen. What should I do?

Try these steps in order:

  1. Quit any app that might be reading USB devices, such as Preview, Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, Android File Transfer, Garmin Express, iMovie or Lightroom.
  2. If Android File Transfer opens every time you connect your phone, uninstall it.

Still stuck? See the troubleshooting guide.

Does OpenMTP work with Samsung phones?

Yes. If the app goes blank when you connect a Samsung phone, uninstall Samsung Smart Switch, which blocks other apps from reaching the phone over MTP.

Can I transfer files larger than 4GB?

Yes. Google's Android File Transfer can't copy files larger than 4GB, but OpenMTP can, and you can transfer many of them in one go.

Can OpenMTP transfer files wirelessly?

No. OpenMTP connects over a USB cable using MTP, which is much faster and more reliable than transferring over Wi-Fi.

Which Macs does OpenMTP support?

OpenMTP runs on macOS 10.15 or later, with native builds for both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

Is a new version of OpenMTP coming?

Yes. The next generation of OpenMTP is in active development and coming soon. It's a major upgrade with new features and big improvements to stability and performance. Stay tuned, and thanks for all the support!
