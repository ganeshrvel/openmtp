How to transfer files from Android to Mac

Three steps. No Wi-Fi setup, no account, no cloud.

  1. 1

    Download OpenMTP

    Get the build for your Mac, Apple Silicon or Intel, and drag OpenMTP into your Applications folder. Or install it with Homebrew.

  2. 2

    Connect your phone with a USB cable

    Unlock your phone, tap the USB notification and choose File transfer. OpenMTP detects it right away.

  3. 3

    Drag and drop your files

    Your Mac is on the left and your phone is on the right. Drag files across, or copy with ⌘C and paste with ⌘V.

Is OpenMTP safe to use?

Yes. OpenMTP is open source under the MIT license, so anyone can read its code. Releases are signed and notarized by Apple, and the app doesn't collect personally identifiable information. Only download it from:

openmtp.ganeshrvel.com GitHub Homebrew

Which Macs does OpenMTP support?

Requires macOS 10.14 (Mojave) or higher. macOS 10.13 and earlier have limited support and require OpenMTP v3.1.15 with Legacy MTP mode.

I'm on macOS 10.13 or earlier. What should I do?

Use OpenMTP v3.1.15 on older Macs, in Legacy MTP mode. Download v3.1.15, then:

  1. Drag the app into the Applications folder.
  2. Turn off the internet.
  3. Launch v3.1.15 of the app.
  4. Go to Settings.
  5. In the Update tab, turn off auto-update.
  6. Turn the internet back on.

Turning off auto-update keeps the app from replacing itself with a newer version your Mac can't run.

Which macOS versions get Kalam kernel updates?

The Kalam kernel brings new device support, fixes and stability improvements. Those updates go to macOS 11 (Big Sur) and later, covering the latest three macOS versions. Kalam has to be compiled on a machine running the oldest macOS it supports, which isn't practical for versions below Big Sur.

On macOS 10.14, OpenMTP still runs, and macOS 10.13 and earlier keep working through Legacy MTP mode in v3.1.15. Releases keep coming for both Intel and Apple Silicon.

My phone isn't connecting, or I see a blank screen. What should I do?

Try these steps in order:

  1. Quit any app that might be reading USB devices, such as Preview, Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, Android File Transfer, Garmin Express, iMovie or Lightroom.
  2. If Android File Transfer opens every time you connect your phone, uninstall it.

Still stuck? See the troubleshooting guide.

How do I show hidden files?

OpenMTP hides dotfiles by default. To see them:

  1. Click the Settings (gear) button above the file listing.
  2. Go to the File Manager tab.
  3. Under Show hidden files, turn on Phone for your device, or Computer for your Mac.
  4. Close Settings and hit refresh above the listing.

How do I switch between internal storage and the SD card?

  1. Connect the device and open it in OpenMTP.
  2. In the right hand pane, click the Storage button above the device file listing.
  3. Pick the storage you want in Select Storage Option, such as the internal storage or the SD card.
  4. Hit refresh if the contents don't appear straight away.

How do I switch between list view and grid view?

  1. Click the Settings (gear) button above the file listing.
  2. Go to the File Manager tab.
  3. Under View as grid, turn it on for grid view or off for list view, separately for Phone and Computer.

List view is the one with checkboxes, so it's the easier way to pick several files, right-click and choose Copy or Copy to Queue.

Apple Silicon or Intel? Which download do I need?

It depends on the chip inside your Mac. Macs sold from late 2020 onwards use Apple Silicon (the M1, M2, M3, M4 and M5 chips). Older ones use Intel processors. OpenMTP ships a separate build for each, so the app runs directly on your chip instead of being translated.

To check which one you have:

  1. Click the Apple menu in the top left.
  2. Choose About This Mac.
  3. Read the Chip or Processor line: a chip starting with Apple M means Apple Silicon, anything saying Intel means the Intel build.

The download buttons on this page already pick the right one for the Mac you're reading this on.

Is OpenMTP a free alternative to Google's Android File Transfer?

Yes. Google has discontinued its Android File Transfer app for Mac, and the popular alternatives are paid or come with restrictions. OpenMTP is free and open source, with no limits.

Does OpenMTP work with Samsung phones?

Yes. If the app goes blank when you connect a Samsung phone, uninstall Samsung Smart Switch, which blocks other apps from reaching the phone over MTP.

Can I transfer files larger than 4GB?

Yes. Google's Android File Transfer can't copy files larger than 4GB, but OpenMTP can, and you can transfer many of them in one go.

Can OpenMTP transfer files wirelessly?

No. OpenMTP connects over a USB cable using MTP, which is much faster and more reliable than transferring over Wi-Fi.

Is a new version of OpenMTP coming?

Yes. The next generation of OpenMTP is in active development and coming soon. It's a major upgrade with new features and big improvements to stability and performance. Stay tuned, and thanks for all the support!
